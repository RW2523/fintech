# Authority matrix

Who may approve what. Authority is about the size of the commitment and the risk it carries, not about how confident anyone feels. A decision above a role's ceiling is not made and then reviewed; it is made by whoever holds the authority.

## Approval ceilings

| Role | Approves up to |
|---|---|
| CREDIT_OFFICER | 20,000 LCU |
| SENIOR_OFFICER | 75,000 LCU |
| CREDIT_COMMITTEE | no ceiling |

### AUT-01

**CREDIT_OFFICER.** This role approves up to 20,000 LCU. A case above that passes to the next authority rather than being split or approved in parts.

### AUT-02

**SENIOR_OFFICER.** This role approves up to 75,000 LCU. A case above that passes to the next authority rather than being split or approved in parts.

### AUT-03

**CREDIT_COMMITTEE.** This role approves any amount, and is the authority for every case above the other ceilings.

### AUT-EX

**Policy exceptions.** An exception to any clause in this policy is approved by SENIOR_OFFICER and by nobody below that role. The exception is recorded against the case with the clause it departs from and the reason, so a later reader can see both what the policy said and why it was set aside.
